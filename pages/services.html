Additional Services

We offer a wide range of specialised cleaning solutions to meet every need.

Retail Cleaning

Keep your shopfront, aisles, and customer areas spotless. We work around trading hours to minimise disruption and maximise presentation.

Fitness Centre Cleaning

Gyms and fitness studios require frequent sanitisation. We clean equipment, change rooms, showers, and high-touch surfaces to maintain hygiene standards.

Medical Facility Cleaning

Hospital-grade disinfection and infection-control cleaning for clinics, dental surgeries, aged care, and healthcare environments. Fully compliant with health regulations.

Periodical ADHOC Cleaning

One-off or periodic deep cleans scheduled on demand. Ideal for post-event cleanups, seasonal refreshes, or pre-inspection preparation.

Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning

Professional steam cleaning and stain removal for carpets, rugs, sofas, and office chairs. Restore fabric to a like-new condition.

Window Cleaning

Crystal-clear results for both interior and exterior windows. Water-fed pole systems and rope access available for multi-storey buildings.

Floor Deep Cleaning

Stripping, sealing, buffing, and polishing for all hard floor types including vinyl, tile, concrete, and timber. Restores shine and extends floor life.
