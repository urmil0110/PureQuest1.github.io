Additional Services

We offer a wide range of specialised cleaning solutions to meet every need.

Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning

Professional steam cleaning and stain removal for carpets, rugs, sofas, and office chairs. Restore fabric to a like-new condition.

Window & Glass Cleaning

Crystal-clear results for both interior and exterior windows. Rope access and water-fed pole systems for multi-storey buildings.

End-of-Lease Cleaning

Bond-back guarantee cleans that meet real estate agency standards. Full property deep-clean including oven, blinds, and walls.

Pressure Washing

Driveways, car parks, building facades, and outdoor areas cleaned with high-pressure equipment. Removes oil, grime, and mould.

Sanitisation & Disinfection

Electrostatic spraying and hospital-grade disinfection for high-touch areas. Ideal for childcare, medical, and food-service environments.

Waste Management

Regular rubbish collection, recycling sorting, and eco-friendly disposal services. Includes confidential document shredding for offices.
